Greece & Rome 800 BCE to 476 CE

Statues that look alive

Greek and Roman sculptors studied real bodies closely so their statues looked incredibly lifelike, some so real that legends say people mistook them for actual humans!

Pictures made of tiny tiles

Romans created mosaics: pictures made from thousands of tiny colored tiles called tesserae. Tap tiles to place them and complete the picture!

The Renaissance 1400 to 1600

Making flat pictures look deep

Renaissance artists discovered perspective: a trick for making a flat painting look like it has real depth, with things farther away appearing smaller. Drag the slider to see the effect!

Impressionism 1860s to 1880s

Up close vs. far away

Impressionist painters like Monet used quick little dabs and dashes of color instead of smooth, careful lines. Drag the slider to zoom out and see what happens!

Van Gogh & Bold Color 1880 to 1890

Painting feelings, not just things

Vincent van Gogh painted with thick, swirling brushstrokes and bold colors to show FEELINGS, not just how things really looked. Tap each color to see what feeling it might paint!

Cubism 1907 to 1920s

Every angle at once

Pablo Picasso helped invent Cubism: painting a face or object from many angles all at the same time, broken into geometric pieces. Tap the face to cubify it!

Tap the face
Abstract Art 1940s to 1950s

Art without any shapes at all

Some artists, like Jackson Pollock, dripped and splattered paint with no recognizable shapes at all, just color, movement, and energy. Tap the canvas to splatter your own!
